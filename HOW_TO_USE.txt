FOLDER TREE VIEWER - How to use
================================

What it does: shows everything inside a folder as a neat, clickable tree.
It only reads folder names. It never changes or deletes anything and needs no internet.

1. Put the app inside the folder you want to look at.
   (Mac: unzip the download first.)
2. Double-click the app.
3. A window opens with that folder's tree. Click a folder to open or close it.

To look at a different folder, just move the app into that folder and double-click again.

Buttons in the window:
  Refresh               - update the view
  Show / hide files     - include or leave out files
  Expand all / Collapse all
  Search box            - find a folder by name

FIRST-TIME WARNINGS (normal, you only see them once)

Windows:
  A blue screen says "Windows protected your PC".
  Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Mac:
  Right-click the app, choose "Open", then click "Open" again.
  If the window says "One quick step": in Finder, drag the app into the folder
  you want to view, then double-click it there.
  If the Mac asks to allow access to Desktop / Documents / Downloads: click Allow.

Works on: Windows 10/11, and Macs with Apple chips (M1 or newer).
